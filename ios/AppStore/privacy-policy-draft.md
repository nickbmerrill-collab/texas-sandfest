# Texas SandFest app privacy policy draft

Status: owner-approved and published at
`https://www.heyelab.com/texas-sandfest/privacy`; the same URL is saved in App
Store Connect.

Effective date: August 14, 2026

Texas SandFest provides a visitor app for event information, schedules,
sculptor information, beach guidance, tickets, and Ask Sandy.

## Information handled by the app

- The app does not require a visitor account or sign-in.
- Favorites, reminders, and imported ticket references are stored on the
  visitor's device.
- When a visitor chooses to ask Sandy a question, the question is sent to the
  Texas SandFest public concierge service so it can return a source-linked
  answer.
- When a visitor chooses to scan a supported ticket QR code, camera access is
  used for that scan. The app does not retain or transmit camera images.
- The app requests approved public event content from the Texas SandFest API.

## Tracking and advertising

The app does not include advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking SDKs, or
third-party analytics SDKs, and Texas SandFest does not use app information to
track visitors across apps or websites owned by other companies.

## Service providers and technical logs

Texas SandFest uses service providers to host the public site and API. Those
providers may process limited technical request information such as an IP
address, request time, requested path, response status, and browser or device
user-agent information to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Texas SandFest may disclose information when required by law, to protect the
safety and integrity of the service, or to service providers working on its
behalf under appropriate restrictions. Texas SandFest does not sell app data.

## Retention and deletion

Information stored only on a visitor's device remains there until the visitor
removes it or deletes the app.

Ask Sandy question text is not stored by the application after the response is
produced. Provider technical logs are retained according to the active hosting
plan and for no longer than 30 days unless a longer period is required to
investigate abuse, protect the service, or comply with law.

## Children

The app is a general-audience event guide and is not designed to collect
personal information from children.

## Contact

Questions or requests about this policy may be sent to
info@texassandfest.org or Texas SandFest, 200 S. Alister Street, Suite E, Port
Aransas, Texas 78373, United States.

## Changes

Texas SandFest may update this policy when the app or its services change. The
published policy will show its current effective date.
