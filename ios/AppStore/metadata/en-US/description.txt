Make the most of Texas SandFest in Port Aransas.

The official visitor experience brings the essentials for a day on the beach together in one native app:

• Check festival dates, hours, location, and current visitor guidance.
• Explore beach conditions and festival zones.
• View the published daily schedule when programming is released.
• Discover participating sculptors after the reviewed roster is published.
• Ask Sandy for source-linked answers about tickets, accessibility, parking, pets, volunteering, sponsors, vendors, and schedule changes.
• Open ticket options and keep supported ticket references handy.
• Continue using the bundled event guide when connectivity is limited.

Texas SandFest is the largest beach sand sculpture competition in the United States and supports local nonprofit organizations and scholarships.

Some live information and event features become available closer to festival weekend. Safety emergencies should always be directed to 911 or on-site emergency personnel.
