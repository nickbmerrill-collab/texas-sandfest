Texas SandFest

Visitor app

Privacy Policy

Effective August 14, 2026

Texas SandFest provides a visitor app for event information, schedules, sculptor information, beach guidance, tickets, and Ask Sandy. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information handled by the app

  • The app does not require a visitor account or sign-in.
  • Favorites, reminders, and imported ticket references are stored on the visitor's device.
  • When a visitor chooses to ask Sandy a question, the question is sent to the Texas SandFest public concierge service so it can return a source-linked answer. Question text is used to answer the request and is not persisted by the application.
  • When a visitor chooses to scan a supported ticket QR code, camera access is used for that scan. The app does not retain or transmit camera images.
  • The app requests approved public event content from the Texas SandFest API.

Tracking and advertising

The app does not include advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking SDKs, or third-party analytics SDKs. Texas SandFest does not use app information to track visitors across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Service providers and technical logs

Texas SandFest uses service providers to host the public site and API. Those providers may process limited technical request information such as an IP address, request time, requested path, response status, and browser or device user-agent information to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the service. Texas SandFest does not sell this information or use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Information stored only on a visitor's device remains there until the visitor removes it or deletes the app. Ask Sandy question text is not stored by the application after the response is produced. Provider technical logs are retained according to the active hosting plan and for no longer than 30 days unless a longer period is required to investigate abuse, protect the service, or comply with law.

Children

The app is a general-audience event guide and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions or requests about this policy may be sent to info@texassandfest.org or to Texas SandFest, 200 S. Alister Street, Suite E, Port Aransas, Texas 78373, United States.

Changes

Texas SandFest may update this policy when the app or its services change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
